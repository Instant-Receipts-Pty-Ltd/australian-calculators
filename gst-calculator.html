How the 1/11th GST rule works

Australia's GST rate is 10%, which works out to 1/11th of any GST-inclusive price. The two examples below show it both ways.

Adding GST

Multiply the GST-exclusive price by 1.1

Base price$340.00
× 1.1+ $34.00 GST
Total (incl. GST)$374.00

Removing GST

Divide the GST-inclusive total by 11

Total (incl. GST)$770.00
÷ 11$70.00 GST
Base price (excl. GST)$700.00

  • Multiply the GST-exclusive price by 1.1 (or add 10% of the price to itself). For example, $340 × 1.1 = $374, meaning $34 of that total is GST.
  • Divide the GST-inclusive total by 11 to find the GST component, then subtract it from the total to get the price excluding GST. For a $770 total: $770 ÷ 11 = $70 GST, so the GST-exclusive price is $700.
