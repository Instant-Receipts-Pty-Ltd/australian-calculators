How to calculate your BAS

Simpler BAS is the default for businesses with a GST turnover under $10 million, and it reports three GST figures. G1 is your total sales. 1A is the GST you charged on those sales. 1B is the GST you paid on business purchases.

Subtract 1B from 1A. A positive answer is what you pay the ATO. A negative answer is a refund.

Try it with these figures

A sole trader with $16,500 of sales and $6,600 of purchases, both including GST.

G1 Total sales$16,500.00
1A GST on sales ($16,500 ÷ 11)$1,500.00
1B GST on purchases ($6,600 ÷ 11)$600.00
GST to pay (1A minus 1B)$900.00

What this calculator leaves out

It only covers the GST labels, and it is for Simpler BAS. If your GST turnover is $10 million or more you use full reporting, which has more labels. Enter only purchases that have GST in the price and that you can claim credits on. If you have staff you may also report PAYG withholding, and you may pay PAYG instalments.

Use the same reporting period for sales and purchases.
