ATO working from home rates

The fixed rate method lets you claim a set amount for each hour you work from home. The rate covers your electricity and gas, phone, internet, stationery and computer consumables, so you do not claim those again.

How to calculate the fixed rate deduction

Add up the hours you worked from home in the year and multiply by the rate. If you worked 20 hours a week from home for 48 weeks in 2025-26, that is 960 hours, and 960 × $0.70 = $672.

Keep a record of your hours for the whole year — a timesheet, roster or diary — and one example of each cost the rate covers. Only hours you actually work at home count.

Anything the rate does not cover, such as a desk, chair, monitor or laptop, can be claimed separately with a receipt.

Fixed rate or actual costs?

The fixed rate is quick and needs few records. The actual cost method means you work out the work share of each bill and keep all of them, so it only pays off when your real extra costs are well above 70 cents an hour. Pick one method for the year.
